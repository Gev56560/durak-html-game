Quick rules

  • Cover an attack with a higher card of the same suit, or any trump.
  • Attack again with a rank already on the table.
  • With 3+ players, transfer by adding a card of the same rank before covering.
  • Empty your hand to escape the fool’s crown.

House rules

Optional party rule: an attacker may hide an added card. The defender can challenge it; a legal card is revealed and stays, an illegal one is taken back.
